Behind the Development

🏗️

Tech for Social Impact

I built Yojana 360 because I believe technology should bridge the gap between complex government systems and the citizens who need them most. My goal was to simplify the lives of rural families and senior citizens in Jabalpur and beyond.

🤖

Built with Modern AI

As an AI-native developer, I leveraged cutting-edge tools to accelerate the development of this platform in just 24 hours during CodeKumbh 2.0.

  • Antigravity: Used for rapid prototyping and interactive development.
  • Gemini & ChatGPT: Acted as my pair-programmers for complex logic and multi-language engine architecture.
🏆

Proudly developed and presented during CodeKumbh 2.0, organized by JLUG (JEC Linux User Group).
